## Core invariants

- Representation is not identical to reality.
- Boundaries are necessary, useful, and dangerous; they are not essence.
- Observation depends on position, conditions, timing, instruments, and questions.
- Invisible, omitted, unregistered, or unrendered does not mean nonexistent.
- A useful model can still be incomplete, and a precise description can still fail a human need.
- OpenCV-like concepts are a translation layer, not the universe's source code.
- Every power must have failure modes and institutional consequences.
- Change should avoid unnecessary irreversible erasure when preservation is possible.
